Our Mission: Total Energy Literacy

To combat the severe effects of climate change across Sri Lanka, every single citizen needs to understand how alternative technologies operate. Explore the core pillars of renewable energy below to discover how we can transition our island away from traditional fossil fuel dependence.

Solar Power

How it battles climate change in SL: Offsets coal usage at Norochcholai, drastically cutting down harmful greenhouse gas emissions and minimizing the intensity of toxic heatwaves hitting our dry zones.

Sri Lankan implementation & locations: Rapidly growing via industrial setups in Hambantota, floating solar trials on Maduru Oya reservoir, and the "Soorya Bala Sangramaya" rooftop network across suburban neighborhoods.

Wind Power

How it battles climate change in SL: Generates high-yield electricity completely emission-free, preserving our delicate coastal ecosystems and reducing the air pollution levels affecting island weather patterns.

Sri Lankan implementation & locations: Utilized heavily at the massive Thambapavani Wind Farm on the Mannar beachfront, alongside active wind turbine corridors stretching through Kalpitiya and Jaffna.

Hydropower

How it battles climate change in SL: Supplies a steady, low-emission baseload of electricity, reducing reliance on emergency diesel power generation during dry-season energy shortfalls.

Sri Lankan implementation & locations: The backbone of the grid for decades, led by the Mahaweli hydro complex including Victoria, Kotmale, and Randenigala dams, along with the Laxapana power stations in the central highlands.

Biomass

How it battles climate change in SL: Converts agricultural and industrial waste into power instead of letting it rot or burn openly, cutting methane release and reducing crop-residue burning in farming regions.

Sri Lankan implementation & locations: Dendro-power plants running on fast-growing Gliricidia in areas like Walapane and Pelawatte, plus rice-husk and sugarcane-waste power projects supporting rural estates.
