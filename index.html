Sri Lanka is drowning. We are drying out. And time is running out.

From unpredictable, devastating floods to prolonged droughts that crush our farmers, climate change is no longer a future threat to Sri Lanka—it is our current reality. Our paradise island is vulnerable, but our greatest weakness is unawareness. Our motive is simple: complete climate literacy. We believe that by educating every citizen on the power of solar, wind, and hydro technologies, we can spark a green revolution. Knowledge is our first line of defense.
